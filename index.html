    🐦‍⬛ Befriending crows

    Crows are too big for hanging feeders, so use a flat tray or the ground. Offer unsalted peanuts in the shell, in the same spot at the same time each day. They will be watching you long before you notice them. Give it a couple of weeks and they'll be waiting for you.

    Golden rules

    Six habits that keep your visitors healthy.

    • 🧼 Keep it clean Wash feeders every one to two weeks and let them dry before refilling. Dirty feeders spread disease.
    • 💧 Always offer water A shallow dish, a few centimetres deep, refreshed daily. In winter, keep it ice-free.
    • 🐈 Mind the cats Place feeders about 2 m from shrubs: close enough for a quick escape, too far for an ambush.
    • 🪟 Mind the windows Put feeders closer than 1 m or farther than 10 m from glass. In between is where the worst collisions happen.
    • 🥄 Little and often Put out only what gets eaten in a day or two, so nothing goes stale or invites rats.
    • 🚫 Never serve Salted food, mouldy food, milk, or fat from the roasting tin. Birds can't handle salt or lactose, and soft fat smears their feathers.
